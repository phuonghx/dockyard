# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want Dockyard to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion
or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect
- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and explain your own.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Be welcoming to beginners. Everyone started somewhere.
- Write in English or Vietnamese; if you use another language, add a short English summary when you can.

## What is not acceptable
- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination or sexualised language or imagery.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information (addresses, emails, phone numbers) without permission.
- Anything that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider harmful.

Report problems privately to **phuonghx.me@gmail.com**. All reports are handled in confidence.

## Scope
This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the
project in public.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
